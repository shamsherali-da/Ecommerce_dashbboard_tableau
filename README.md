# 📊 Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive and visually rich dashboard for analyzing Year-To-Date (YTD) ecommerce performance across multiple regions and product categories. Built to support strategic decision-making by tracking key metrics like sales, profit, order quantity, market share, and category trends.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive snapshot of ecommerce performance using dynamic visualizations. It helps stakeholders monitor growth, identify underperforming categories, and optimize regional strategies.

---

## 📈 Key Features

- **Sales Overview**: Total YTD sales, profit, and order quantity with monthly trend lines and YoY comparisons.
- **Market Share Analysis**: Pie chart showing regional contribution to total sales (Europe, LATAM, Pacific Asia, Africa).
- **Category Performance**: Bar chart comparing YTD vs PYTD sales across 10+ product categories with color-coded margins.
- **Monthly Acquisition Trends**: Line chart ranking market-wise acquisition activity from January to December.
- **Color-coded Insights**:
  - 🟩 Green: Growth vs Previous Year
  - 🟥 Red: Decline vs Previous Year
  - 🔵 Blue: No sales in Previous Year

---

## 📊 Visuals Included

- Line charts for monthly trends in sales, profit, and order quantity
- Pie chart for market share breakdown
- Bar chart for category-wise performance
- Multi-line chart for monthly acquisition rankings by region
